# NVC and ILD in connective tissue diseases other than SSc: what is known and what is missing

Search basis: PubMed (MCP tool plus NCBI E-utilities; queries combining capillaroscopy/nailfold with myositis, dermatomyositis, antisynthetase, MDA5, rapidly progressive, IPAF, interstitial lung disease, MCTD, UCTD, rheumatoid, lupus, Sjögren, China/Chinese). About 400 records were screened by title. Abstracts were read for about 70 of them. Each citation below gives PMID, DOI, design, n and the key finding from the abstract. I did not read full texts, so where a fact only appears in the full text it is listed as a gap. The one web source (on IPAF criteria content) is secondary and is marked as such.

---

## 1. IIM / antisynthetase syndrome (ASS): prevalence of NVC abnormalities, association with ILD, Jo1 vs non-Jo1, and longitudinal/prognostic data

### Takeaway
NVC abnormalities are common in DM, ADM/CADM and overlap myositis. The scleroderma or "SSc-like" pattern is seen in roughly 45–80% of DM in most series. Rates are lower in ASS (overall abnormality about 60%; SSc-like pattern about 35%) and NVC is usually normal or non-specific in PM, IMNM and IBM. The data on ILD are mixed. Several cross-sectional studies link capillary loss, avascular areas and microhaemorrhages to ILD presence and to lower FVC/DLCO. Other studies find no link with lung involvement. In ASS/Jo1 the commonest pattern is non-specific. No study has prospectively tested NVC as a predictor of ILD progression (FVC decline or HRCT progression) in IIM. The only survival analyses are retrospective and small, and they are either negative (Xu 2025) or limited to ILD-related death in a small anti-MDA5/ARS cohort (Wakura 2020).

### Cited Findings
**Prevalence and patterns**
- **Bogojevic M et al., J Clin Med 2024.** PMID 39337037, DOI 10.3390/jcm13185550. Cross-sectional, Belgrade, n=90 IIM (37 DM, 35 PM, 13 ADM, 5 JDM) plus 35 primary RP controls. Scleroderma pattern in 81.9% of DM, 76.9% of ADM and 80% of JDM. In PM the commonest finding was non-specific change (48.6%). The scleroderma pattern correlated with Gottron's, heliotrope, periungual erythema, RP **and ILD**. Anti-Jo1 was more frequent in patients with non-specific changes. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39337037/)
- **Torres-Ruiz J et al., Clin Exp Rheumatol 2024.** PMID 38488092, DOI 10.55563/clinexprheumatol/l9gudh. Multicentre, multi-ethnic (Mexico, Spain, USA), n=155 IIM (61.2% DM). NVC was normal in only 9%. A non-specific pattern was the most frequent (53.9%) and was **associated with ILD, Jo1, ASS, joint involvement and IMNM**. The SSc pattern was associated with DM/overlap, cutaneous features and anti-TIF1γ. In 23 patients with repeat NVC after disease control, the capillaroscopic score, capillary diameter and avascular areas decreased and capillary density increased. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38488092/)
- **Sebastiani M et al., J Rheumatol 2019.** PMID 30442823, DOI 10.3899/jrheum.180355. Retrospective, multicentre international, n=190 ASS (133 anti-Jo1, 57 non-Jo1) vs 75 primary RP. NVC abnormalities in 62.1% vs 29.3% (p<0.001). SSc-like pattern in 35.3%, **associated with anti-Jo1 (p=0.002)** and longer disease duration (p=0.004). Only 47% of patients with the SSc-like pattern had RP. The abstract reports no ILD-outcome association. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30442823/)
- **Mugii N et al., J Dermatol 2026.** PMID 41312673, DOI 10.1111/1346-8138.70081. Retrospective, Japan, n=66 ASS across six anti-ARS subtypes. Anti-KS patients had milder abnormalities (haemorrhages 25% vs 75–100% in the other subtypes). — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41312673/)
- **Leclair V et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2026.** PMID 41206056, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keaf594. Prospective Canadian Inflammatory Myopathy Study cohort, n=99. Decreased capillary density in 95% of DM, 92% of scleromyositis and 87% of ASS. The NVC pattern did not discriminate DM, scleromyositis and ASS from one another. NVC was normal in IMNM, IBM and PM. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41206056/)
- **Kubo S et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2019.** PMID 30169697, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/key257. Prospective, n=70 untreated Japanese IIM. NVC abnormal in 65.4% of DM vs 27.8% of PM. Abnormal in **87.5% of anti-MDA5, 88.9% of anti-TIF1γ and 26.9% of anti-ARS** (p<0.001). Abnormalities **disappeared in 75% after 1 year of immunosuppression**, whereas SSc changes are stable. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30169697/)
- **Soubrier C et al., Clin Rheumatol 2019.** PMID 31440919, DOI 10.1007/s10067-019-04710-2. Cross-sectional, n=43 (17 DM, 8 overlap, 12 ASS, 6 IMNM). Giant capillaries, disorganisation and avascular zones were seen only in DM and overlap myositis. A scleroderma pattern was seen only in overlap myositis. ASS and IMNM had no giant capillaries. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31440919/)
- **Piette Y et al. (EULAR SG on Microcirculation), Autoimmun Rev 2022.** PMID 35421608, DOI 10.1016/j.autrev.2022.103087. Systematic review: of 653 records, only **5 studies** passed quality appraisal. NVC differed markedly between (j)DM and PM, healthy controls and SSc. Reduced density and the scleroderma pattern seemed to be associated with active (j)DM, and immunosuppression appears to reduce abnormalities. The authors call NVC "a promising asset" and a possible biomarker for organ involvement and follow-up. They state that "large multicentre prospective standardised studies are further needed". — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35421608/)
- **Flatley EM et al., Arch Dermatol Res 2024.** PMID 39565371, DOI 10.1007/s00403-024-03521-z. Systematic review of nailfold findings in DM: 37 papers, 346 patients. Scleroderma-spectrum pattern in 45.1%. Only 51 patients had NVC data linked to antibodies. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39565371/)
- **Hysa E et al., RMD Open 2023.** PMID 37451812, DOI 10.1136/rmdopen-2023-003071. Genoa EULAR centre, retrospective, n=1181 CTD with secondary RP. Giant capillaries in 73% of SSc, 73% of DM and 61% of MCTD. Capillary count was lowest in SSc, DM and MCTD. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37451812/)

**Association with ILD presence and severity (cross-sectional)**
- **Sieiro Santos C et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2025.** PMID 39658251, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keae669. Retrospective, single reference centre, n=95 IIM. ILD was associated with **avascular zones (p=0.004), capillary loss (p=0.04) and microhaemorrhages (p=0.04)**. Capillary loss and enlarged capillaries correlated negatively with %FVC (rs −0.46 and −0.57) and %DLCO (rs −0.32 and −0.23). sKL-6 correlated with avascular areas (rs 0.64) and microhaemorrhages. Cross-sectional only. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39658251/)
- **Selva-O'Callaghan A et al., Semin Arthritis Rheum 2010.** PMID 19022479, DOI 10.1016/j.semarthrit.2008.09.003. n=53 adult IIM. ILD correlated with a higher capillary score (**OR 3.7, 95% CI 1.1–13.0**). Activity and severity were associated with more NVC findings. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19022479/)
- **Mugii N et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2011.** PMID 21258053, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keq430. n=50 adult DM. Scleroderma pattern in 74%. Capillary loss tracked muscle and global activity, and haemorrhages tracked skin activity. **NVC was not significantly associated with lung involvement.** Several scores fell after treatment. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21258053/); this contradicts Sieiro Santos 2025 and Selva-O'Callaghan 2010.
- **Xu H et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2025** (China, Nantong). PMID 39689020, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keae677. Retrospective, n=76 DM plus 26 ASS and 33 SSc controls, 2017–2022, with follow-up to Dec 2022. NVC abnormal in 73.7% of DM. DM NVC was milder than SSc but more abnormal than ASS. Hierarchical clustering on NVC gave 2 clusters: **cluster 1 was more likely to develop ILD, but survival did not differ between clusters.** — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39689020/)
- **Bogojevic 2024** (above): scleroderma pattern correlated with ILD. **Torres-Ruiz 2024** (above): the *non-specific* pattern, not the SSc pattern, was associated with ILD. The two studies conflict on which pattern carries the ILD association, probably because ASS/Jo1 patients make up different shares of each cohort.
- **Juvenile DM.** Barth Z et al., Arthritis Care Res 2019. PMID 29953739, DOI 10.1002/acr.23687. n=58 JDM, 17 years after onset. Low capillary density (<6/mm) was associated with lower FVC and TLC and more often low DLCO (71% vs 38%); this was mostly subclinical. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29953739/). Also a juvenile CTD cohort, Clin Exp Rheumatol 2026 (PMID 41487110, DOI 10.55563/clinexprheumatol/6bz7ok; n=50: JSSc, JDM, JSLE, pRP): higher avascular score in ILD (0.69 vs 0.44, p=0.048), and density ≤4/mm was more often seen with ILD. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41487110/)
- **Dermoscopy/NFC in DM**, Acta Dermatovenerol APA 2024. PMID 38918941 (no DOI in PubMed). n=43 DM, 8 with ILD. Prominent subpapillary venous plexus was associated with pulmonary involvement. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38918941/)

**Does NVC change with activity or treatment in DM? (longitudinal)**
- **Johnson D et al., Front Med 2021.** PMID 34447769, DOI 10.3389/fmed.2021.708432. Prospective, n=15 DM, 2 visits at least 9 months apart. Capillary density improved from 4.71 to 6.84 per mm in step with MITAX. Density was independently associated with MITAX (β −1.49); CRP and CK were not. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34447769/)
- **Mugii N et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2023.** PMID 35866689, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keac401. Retrospective, n=71 (25 MDA5, 24 TIF1, 22 ARS). After stabilisation, enlarged and reduced capillaries improved **only in anti-MDA5**, not in TIF1 or ARS. Haemorrhages decreased in all three groups. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35866689/)
- **Miossi R et al., Clin Rheumatol 2019.** PMID 31016577, DOI 10.1007/s10067-019-04537-x. Prospective, n=23 new-onset DM, 15 re-assessed at 3.2 years. All NVC parameters improved except enlarged and bushy capillaries. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31016577/)
- **Pizzorni C et al., Clin Rheumatol 2018.** PMID 30006917, DOI 10.1007/s10067-018-4211-2. Retrospective, n=24 DM vs 24 SSc, 3-year follow-up. Scleroderma-like pattern in 79% of DM. **No significant change in NVC scores over 3 years**. Anti-Jo1 did not change the pattern. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30006917/); this conflicts with Kubo 2019 and Johnson 2021.
- **Mercer LK et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2010.** PMID 20483911, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keq051. Quantitative NVC, n=24 IIM, 16 re-tested at 6–12 months. Anti-Jo1 was associated with reduced density. The change in density was −1.4 vs −0.4 in controls (p=0.07). — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20483911/)
- **Shenavandeh S, Rashidi F, Reumatologia 2022.** PMID 35645422, DOI 10.5114/reum.2022.114109. Cross-sectional, n=150 IIM. Scleroderma pattern in 72.8% of DM, 76% of ADM and 78.4% of overlap myositis. It was associated with skin VAS (and with global VAS in overlap myositis). — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35645422/)

**Interferon link (China)**
- **Tang M et al., Arthritis Res Ther 2025** (PUMCH, Beijing). PMID 40114239, DOI 10.1186/s13075-025-03532-9. Cross-sectional, n=55 IIM (MDA5+, ARS+, MSA−). The MDA5+ group had higher dilation, giant capillaries, microhaemorrhage and abnormal morphology scores than ARS+. NVC scores correlated with ISGs (MX1, IFI27) and IP-10, RANTES and GROα. No ILD-outcome analysis is reported in the abstract. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40114239/)

### Inferences
- Within IIM, the NVC phenotype splits roughly as follows. DM, MDA5 and TIF1γ show active, florid, often reversible change: microhaemorrhages, enlarged and giant capillaries, ramification. ASS/ARS shows milder, non-specific change, with the SSc-like pattern concentrated in Jo1 and long disease duration. Because ILD is concentrated in ASS and MDA5, any NVC–ILD association in a pooled IIM cohort is confounded by the antibody subgroup. A new study should stratify by MSA, or adjust for it.
- DM NVC changes are dynamic and treatment-responsive in most series (Kubo, Johnson, Miossi, Torres-Ruiz, Mugii 2023), unlike in SSc. One small series disagrees (Pizzorni 2018). This makes serial NVC a plausible activity or response marker. It also means a single baseline NVC may be a weaker predictor of long-term lung fibrosis than in SSc.
- Capillary loss, avascular areas and microhaemorrhage are the parameters most often linked to ILD and FVC/DLCO (Sieiro Santos 2025, juvenile cohorts, Wakura 2020). They are the logical candidate predictors to pre-specify.

### Gaps
- I found no prospective IIM/ASS study that tests baseline NVC (or its change) against **ILD progression** defined by FVC decline ≥10%, DLCO decline, quantitative HRCT change or the progressive pulmonary fibrosis (PPF) criteria.
- No survival analysis of NVC in ASS-ILD was found. Xu 2025 found no survival difference in DM. Its survival endpoint was all-cause and not ILD-specific, according to the abstract.
- Jo1 vs non-Jo1 (PL-7, PL-12, EJ, OJ, KS) NVC data exist (Sebastiani 2019, Mugii 2026), but none is linked to ILD outcomes.
- I could not verify from the abstracts whether Sebastiani 2019 or Torres-Ruiz 2024 analysed ILD severity (FVC, HRCT extent) or only ILD presence. That would need the full texts.

## 2. Anti-MDA5 DM / CADM: NVC data relating to RP-ILD or mortality

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin, all from Japan or China, and all small. The most relevant study is **Wakura 2020** (Japan): in DM-ILD, the microhaemorrhage score was higher in patients who died of ILD and correlated with known poor-prognosis factors and with HRCT fibrosis scores. Anti-MDA5 patients have florid but **reversible** NVC changes that normalise within weeks to months of treatment. One prospective study (Sugimoto 2022, n=10) found an *inverse* correlation between NVC score and anti-MDA5 titre, and a positive correlation between NVC score and FVC. The direction of the NVC–severity relationship in MDA5 is therefore unsettled. No study has specifically evaluated NVC as a predictor of RP-ILD onset or 3- or 6-month mortality in an anti-MDA5 cohort.

### Cited Findings
- **Wakura R et al., Sci Rep 2020.** PMID 32973255, DOI 10.1038/s41598-020-72752-7. Comparative, DM-ILD, anti-MDA5 vs anti-ARS (the sample size is not stated in the abstract). MDA5 had higher microhaemorrhage (p=0.012) and capillary disorganisation (p=0.044) scores, and ARS had more tortuous capillaries (p=0.002). **The microhaemorrhage score was higher in the ILD-related-death group than in survivors (p=0.02).** Microhaemorrhage, disorganisation and neoangiogenesis correlated with known poor-prognosis factors of DM-ILD. Microhaemorrhage and capillary loss correlated with the HRCT total fibrosis score. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32973255/)
- **Sugimoto T et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2022.** PMID 34498053, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keab681. Prospective, n=10 MDA5+ DM/CADM. NVC abnormalities improved in all patients 2–17 weeks after immunosuppression began. **Baseline NVC scores were inversely correlated with anti-MDA5 titres, and NVC score correlated positively with FVC.** — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34498053/). Two letters commented on it (PMIDs 34730797, 34726739; their content was not read).
- **Hamaguchi Y et al., J Dermatol 2021.** PMID 32902854, DOI 10.1111/1346-8138.15589. n=11 MDA5+ DM, before and after treatment. Irregularly enlarged capillaries fell from 91% to 18% and haemorrhages from 100% to 0%. Serum FGF23 rose after treatment. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32902854/)
- **Mugii 2023** (PMID 35866689, above): enlarged and reduced capillaries improved only in the MDA5 group.
- **Kubo 2019** (PMID 30169697, above): NVC abnormal in 87.5% of anti-MDA5.
- **Tang 2025** (PMID 40114239, above, China): MDA5+ patients had the most florid NVC, correlated with the IFN signature.
- Case reports: MDA5 RP-ILD with SSc-pattern capillaroscopy (PMID 29665800, DOI 10.1186/s12890-018-0622-8 — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29665800/)); paediatric MDA5 RP-ILD with nailfold capillary dropout (PMID 40514742, DOI 10.1186/s13256-025-05336-6 — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40514742/)).

### Inferences
- In anti-MDA5 DM, NVC may track the vasculopathic or interferon-driven activity component (Tang 2025; reversibility). Microhaemorrhage is the leading candidate marker for adverse ILD outcome (Wakura 2020). Sugimoto's inverse titre correlation is a warning that "more NVC abnormality = worse" may not hold in MDA5. A study should model individual parameters, not a global pattern.
- MDA5 RP-ILD is common in Chinese and East Asian cohorts. That makes a Chinese prospective cohort well placed to test NVC (baseline plus early serial measurements) for RP-ILD and 6-month mortality, alongside ferritin, KL-6, anti-MDA5 titre and HRCT score.

### Gaps
- No anti-MDA5 cohort has tested NVC for **RP-ILD occurrence** or **mortality** with time-to-event analysis, multivariable adjustment (e.g. against the FLAIR or MCH score, ferritin, titre) or ROC/C-statistics.
- No data were found on NVC in **CADM specifically** in relation to lung outcomes, beyond being part of the MDA5 or ADM groups.
- The sample size of Wakura 2020 and its exact mortality numbers are not in the abstract.

## 3. IPAF / UCTD and ILD clinics: NVC in the IPAF criteria, and evidence on outcomes

### Takeaway
**NVC is not one of the 2015 ERS/ATS IPAF clinical-domain items.** The clinical domain covers mechanic's hands, digital tip ulceration, inflammatory arthritis, palmar telangiectasia, Raynaud's, unexplained digital oedema and Gottron's sign. According to a secondary source, the task force listed the role of NVC among unresolved issues. In ILD clinics, NVC raises the identification of CTD-ILD. A meta-analysis (Umashankar 2022) found abnormal NVC in about 80% of CTD-ILD, about 27% of IPAF and about 14% of IPF, with low-quality evidence. Data on prognosis are almost absent. Acemoğlu 2024 found NVC could not separate non-SSc CTD-ILD from non-CTD ILD. Two IPAF cohorts (Decker 2022; Sambataro 2023) suggest capillaroscopy abnormalities may flag progression to a definite CTD. No study links NVC to IPAF lung progression or survival.

### Cited Findings
- **IPAF criteria:** Fischer A et al., ERS/ATS research statement, Eur Respir J 2015. PMID 26160873, DOI 10.1183/13993003.00150-2015 — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26160873/). The clinical-domain content and the statement that NVC is not an item (it is listed as an unresolved issue) come from a web-search summary of secondary sources. I could not open the full text (the PDF download was blocked) — [secondary: AHN IPAF symposium slides](https://cme.ahn.org/sites/default/files/course/2024-11/08.%20IPAFPCCMsymposium2024BDiSilvio.pdf); [ERS Eur Respir Rev 2021 review](https://publications.ersnet.org/lookup/doi/10.1183/16000617.0177-2021).
- **Umashankar E et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2022.** PMID 34668513, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keab772. Systematic review and meta-analysis, 21 studies. Prevalence of NVC abnormality: **CTD-ILD 80.4% (74.3–85.3), IPAF 27.4% (10.9–53.7), IPF 13.8% (5.7–29.9)**. The late scleroderma pattern was most prevalent in CTD-ILD (40.4%). GRADE quality was low. The authors say future research is needed on the **prognostic value** of NVC in ILD. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34668513/)
- **Acemoğlu ŞŞZ et al., Clin Rheumatol 2024.** PMID 38963466, DOI 10.1007/s10067-024-07049-5. Cross-sectional, n=95 ILD (49 non-CTD, 46 CTD-ILD). Abnormal morphology occurred in both groups, and NVC "**does not distinguish the non-SSc etiology of ILD**". Tortuosity was higher in UIP than NSIP (p=0.041) and increased with disease duration. The authors suggest NVC may help prognosis in IPF; this is speculative. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38963466/)
- **Lee SH et al., Korean J Intern Med 2023.** PMID 37488834, DOI 10.3904/kjim.2022.358. n=81 newly diagnosed ILD (31 CTD-ILD, 18 IPAF, 32 IIP). Semi-quantitative score 5.8 vs 4.2 vs 3.0 (p<0.001). Scleroderma pattern in 27.8% of IPAF and 0% of IIP. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37488834/)
- **Sabella DVA et al., Reumatismo 2026.** PMID 42922420, DOI 10.4081/reumatismo.2026.2398. Cross-sectional, n=67 (16 IPF, 13 IPAF, 38 CTD-ILD). Total score 3.2 vs 4.5 vs 6.5 (p=0.001). Scleroderma pattern 6.3% / 23.1% / 65.7%. **Non-specific abnormalities in 81.3% of IPF.** — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42922420/)
- **Jee AS et al., ERJ Open Res 2021.** PMID 34760999, DOI 10.1183/23120541.00416-2021. Prospective, smartphone-dermatoscope NFC, n=96 ILD (27 CTD-ILD, 42 IIP, 27 IPAF). Low density (<6/mm), giant capillaries (≥3), avascular areas (≥2) and microhaemorrhages discriminated CTD-ILD (OR 5.0–7.5). Microhaemorrhages had adjusted OR 13.45. This is a diagnostic study, not a prognostic one. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34760999/)
- **Sambataro D et al., Diagnostics 2020.** PMID 32344844, DOI 10.3390/diagnostics10050253. n=361 consecutive ILD. NVC positive in 17.7% of all ILD and 78.1% of CTD-ILD. NVC was necessary for the diagnosis in 25% of SSc-ILD. Bushy capillaries and NVC positivity with normal CK pointed to amyopathic IIM, regardless of RP. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32344844/)
- **Romero-Bueno FI et al., Front Med 2023.** PMID 36873897, DOI 10.3389/fmed.2023.1057643. Retrospective, n=118 autoimmune IP. NVC abnormal in 54% of IPAF and 36% of undifferentiated autoimmune IP, often without RP. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36873897/)
- **Decker P et al., Eur J Intern Med 2022.** PMID 34953656, DOI 10.1016/j.ejim.2021.12.021. Multicentre retrospective, 70 IPAF vs 179 non-IPAF, mean follow-up 77 months. 26% of IPAF developed a CTD (9 ASS, 8 SSc). Those who progressed "**more frequently [had] puffy fingers, capillaroscopy abnormalities and antisynthetase antibodies at diagnosis**". This was a trend; the abstract gives no test statistic. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34953656/)
- **Sambataro G et al., Clin Exp Rheumatol 2023.** PMID 36189910, DOI 10.55563/clinexprheumatol/lycdca. Prospective multicentre, n=191 IPAF with yearly capillaroscopy and HRCT. 24.1% progressed to a systemic autoimmune disease. "No parameters were associated with overall progression." Specific predictors included palmar telangiectasias and anti-Scl70 for SSc, and mechanic's hands and anti-MDA5 for IIM. **The abstract reports no NVC predictor and no lung-function or survival analysis tied to NVC.** — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36189910/)
- **Ahmad K et al., Respir Med 2017.** PMID 28137497, DOI 10.1016/j.rmed.2016.10.017. n=57 IPAF. Giant capillaries in 13/30 tested (the abstract reports this as "23%", which is inconsistent with 13/30 = 43%). Survival was similar to IPF, but NVC was not analysed against survival. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28137497/)
- **Sambataro G et al., Respir Med 2019.** PMID 30961944, DOI 10.1016/j.rmed.2019.03.011. Prospective cohort, 45 IPAF vs 143 IPF, all had NVC. Results on NVC are not detailed in the abstract. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30961944/)
- **Canofari C et al., Mediterr J Rheumatol 2023.** PMID 37654644, DOI 10.31138/mjr.34.2.180. n=36 IPAF. Non-specific microangiopathy was the commonest NVC pattern, especially in UIP. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37654644/)
- **Tirelli C et al., Front Med 2020.** PMID 32133362, DOI 10.3389/fmed.2020.00011. An MDT strategy with routine NVC in all incident ILD found 15% CTD and 33% IPAF. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32133362/)
- **UCTD vs MCTD:** Pizzorni C et al., Microvasc Res 2022. PMID 35364109, DOI 10.1016/j.mvr.2022.104367. SSc-like pattern in 48% of MCTD vs 11% of UCTD. Non-specific changes in 83% of UCTD. ILD was not analysed in the abstract. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35364109/)
- **van Roon AM et al., J Rheumatol 2019.** PMID 30554151, DOI 10.3899/jrheum.180615. n=759 RP patients, mixed CTDs. SSc pattern in 33% of pSS, 17% of SLE, 71% of MCTD and 13% of RA. **An SSc pattern was associated with abnormal PFT (FVC or DLCO <70%: 35.9% vs 19.5%, p=0.002), independent of an SSc diagnosis.** NPV 88%. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30554151/)

### Inferences
- Because NVC is not formally an IPAF criterion, a prospective study testing whether NVC (a) reclassifies IPAF patients, (b) predicts progression to definite CTD, or (c) predicts lung progression or PPF would answer a question the IPAF task force explicitly left open.
- Non-specific abnormalities are common even in IPF (Sabella 2026: 81%; Acemoğlu 2024). Any outcome study therefore needs quantitative parameters (density, avascular areas, microhaemorrhages) rather than "abnormal vs normal" as the exposure. It should also include an IPF/IIP comparator or adjust for fibrosis pattern (UIP vs NSIP).

### Gaps
- No IPAF study has tested NVC as a predictor of **FVC/DLCO decline, HRCT progression, PPF, acute exacerbation or mortality**.
- Decker 2022 reports the capillaroscopy–CTD-evolution signal only as a trend. Sambataro 2023 found no overall predictor, and whether NVC was formally tested could not be confirmed from the abstract.
- The Umashankar meta-analysis was limited to prevalence and diagnosis; no pooled prognostic estimate exists.

## 4. MCTD, RA-ILD, SLE, Sjögren's: NVC–ILD association data

### Takeaway
**MCTD** has the most data after IIM. Severe capillary loss (≤4/mm), avascular areas and neoangiogenesis are associated with ILD presence (Kasser 2025; Diógenes 2007; Niklas 2022). NVC also predicts PAH and progression to a differentiated CTD (Todoroki 2022; Chevalier 2024). Patterns may regress with immunosuppression. There are **no** longitudinal NVC → ILD-progression data. **SLE** has old physiological data linking NVC abnormality to reduced DLCO (Groen 1992) and paediatric data linking the scleroderma pattern to damage. There are no ILD outcome studies. **Sjögren's** has one positive study for density–ILD in a 2020 EULAR systematic review (7 quality studies in total) and a small Turkish study. **RA-ILD** has essentially one small study (Karadoğan 2015, n=7 RA-ILD).

### Cited Findings
**MCTD**
- **Kasser C et al., Clin Exp Rheumatol 2025.** PMID 39711361, DOI 10.55563/clinexprheumatol/xshsd7. Cochin, n=51 MCTD (31% ILD), 40 with NVC. Patterns: normal 15%, non-specific 40%, scleroderma 45%. ILD vs no ILD: capillary count 4.80 vs 6.03/mm (p=0.039). Density ≤4/mm with ILD 5/7 vs 5/33 (p=0.002). Neoangiogenesis 6/13 vs 4/27 (p=0.034). These held **independently of the scleroderma pattern and skin sclerosis**. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39711361/)
- **de Holanda Mafaldo Diógenes A et al., Lupus 2007.** PMID 17439931, DOI 10.1177/0961203307076517. n=63 MCTD with NVC 5 years apart. 76% of patients with ILD had avascular areas (p=0.017). 16% changed pattern, 7 of whom normalised. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17439931/)
- **Rajendran A et al., Mediterr J Rheumatol 2024** (India). PMID 39211021, DOI 10.31138/mjr.260423.cnv. 27 MCTD-ILD vs 27 SSc-ILD. Capillary loss was associated with lower FVC, and spirometric restriction severity was associated with NFC grade. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39211021/)
- **Niklas K et al., Postepy Dermatol Alergol 2022.** PMID 36457689, DOI 10.5114/ada.2022.120882. 61 SSc plus 19 MCTD, pooled. Avascular areas correlated with lung fibrosis on HRCT. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36457689/)
- **Todoroki Y et al., Rheumatology (Oxford) 2022.** PMID 35285493, DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keac165. Prospective multicentre, n=50 untreated MCTD with 1-year follow-up. Scleroderma pattern in 38%; it was present in all 11 MCTD patients with PAH vs 21% of those without. **The pattern disappeared in half after immunosuppression.** ILD was not reported in the abstract. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35285493/)
- **Chevalier K et al., J Intern Med 2024.** PMID 38013625, DOI 10.1111/joim.13752. French multicentre retrospective, n=330 MCTD, median follow-up 8 years; 27.9% ILD, 5.6% died. Abnormal NVC at diagnosis predicted progression to a differentiated CTD (**OR 2.44, 1.11–5.58**). An NVC–ILD association was not reported in the abstract. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38013625/)
- **Paolino S et al., Clin Exp Rheumatol 2019.** PMID 31587695 (no DOI listed). n=10 MCTD, 3-year follow-up. NVC was not progressive and did not correlate with clinical aspects. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31587695/)
- **Ornowska S et al., Clin Rheumatol 2024.** PMID 38509242, DOI 10.1007/s10067-024-06879-7. n=43 MCTD. SD-like pattern in 46.5%. Associations were found with sclerodactyly and PAH; no ILD association was reported. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38509242/)
- **Houtman PM, Kallenberg CG, J Rheumatol 1992** (letter). PMID 1433025. Title: "Nailfold capillary density as a possible indicator of pulmonary capillary loss in SLE but not in MCTD". — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1433025/)

**SLE**
- **Groen H et al., Am J Med 1992.** PMID 1466357, DOI 10.1016/0002-9343(92)90194-g. n=57 SLE. Nailfold capillary abnormalities correlated with decreased TLCO and Dm (membrane diffusing capacity). The ILD subset had scleroderma traits and anti-U1RNP. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1466357/)
- **Schonenberg-Meinema D et al., Lupus Sci Med 2022.** PMID 35140136, DOI 10.1136/lupus-2021-000572. Longitudinal cSLE, n=53. Scleroderma pattern ever in 18.9%, associated with anti-RNP and SLICC damage (HR 4.5, 1.1–18.8). Lung data are not stated. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35140136/)
- **Chebbi PP et al., Rheumatol Int 2022.** PMID 34076720, DOI 10.1007/s00296-021-04894-4. RNP+ SLE had more abnormal NVC. The density difference was independent of RP, ILD and disease duration; ILD was used as a covariate, not an outcome. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34076720/)
- **Makarem YS et al., Reumatol Clin 2025.** PMID 40316396, DOI 10.1016/j.reumae.2025.501840. n=86 SLE. NVC was associated with SLEDAI, with no lung endpoints. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40316396/)

**Sjögren's**
- **Melsens K et al. (EULAR SG MC/RD), Clin Exp Rheumatol 2020.** PMID 33025880. Systematic review: 7 quality studies. Capillary density was associated with ILD **in one study** only. A call for prospective follow-up studies. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33025880/)

**RA-ILD (and SjS-ILD)**
- **Çakmakçı Karadoğan D et al., Tuberk Toraks 2015.** PMID 25849052, DOI 10.5578/tt.8673. 15 CTD-ILD (8 SjS-ILD, 7 RA-ILD), 18 idiopathic ILD and 17 CTD controls without ILD, none with RP. Density was reduced in CTD-ILD vs controls (p=0.006). RA-ILD, SjS-ILD and IPF had more severe abnormality. **UIP had lower density than NSIP.** — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25849052/)
- **van Roon 2019** (above): SSc pattern in 13% of RA, 33% of pSS and 17% of SLE among patients with RP. The SSc pattern was associated with abnormal PFT across diagnoses.
- **Anghel D et al., Diagnostics 2025** (narrative review). PMID 39941292, DOI 10.3390/diagnostics15030362. Claims low capillary density is associated with ILD presence and severity and lower FVC/DLCO across CTDs, and that NVC can separate UIP from NSIP. It calls for further research. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39941292/)

### Inferences
- MCTD shows the same signal as SSc and IIM: capillary loss and avascular areas go with ILD. MCTD NVC also responds to immunosuppression (Todoroki 2022) and is "dynamic" (Diógenes 2007). Serial NVC versus ILD trajectory in MCTD is untested and feasible.
- The UIP-associated lower density and tortuosity (Karadoğan 2015; Acemoğlu 2024) raise the possibility that fibrosis itself, or age and smoking, drives some NVC change. RA-ILD, which is UIP-predominant, would therefore be a useful test of whether NVC reflects autoimmune microangiopathy or the fibrotic phenotype.

### Gaps
- **RA-ILD:** I found no dedicated cohort (beyond n=7) and no longitudinal data.
- **SLE-ILD:** there are no modern NVC–ILD studies; the latest physiological data date from 1992.
- **Sjögren-ILD:** there is no longitudinal or outcome study. The 2020 EULAR review found one cross-sectional density–ILD association.
- **MCTD-ILD:** there are cross-sectional associations only. Chevalier 2024 used NVC for evolution to a differentiated CTD, not lung outcome.

## 5. Chinese cohorts and journals indexed in PubMed

### Takeaway
PubMed-indexed Chinese NVC work on non-SSc CTD-ILD is sparse. It consists of a Nantong DM cohort (Xu 2025), a PUMCH IIM/IFN study (Tang 2025), one ocular-OCTA CTD-ILD study (not NVC), and a Beijing SSc-ILD paper that is out of scope. I found no Chinese-language (Zhonghua) PubMed-indexed NVC–ILD paper for IIM, MCTD, RA, SLE, SjS or IPAF; the search returned nothing. Japanese data dominate the anti-MDA5 NVC literature.

### Cited Findings
- **Xu H et al. 2025** (Nantong; PMID 39689020): an NVC cluster was associated with ILD, but not with survival.
- **Tang M et al. 2025** (PUMCH; PMID 40114239): MDA5+ had the most florid NVC, correlated with ISGs and cytokines. Lung outcomes are not in the abstract.
- **Chen LM et al., Int J Ophthalmol 2024.** PMID 39430022, DOI 10.18240/ijo.2024.10.14. OCTA in 16 CTD-ILD vs 16 controls: reduced retinal microvascular density. This is an alternative microvascular readout, not NVC. — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39430022/)
- **Beijing Da Xue Xue Bao 2018.** PMID 29930420: "Correlation between nailfold capillaroscopic findings and presence of interstitial lung disease in systemic sclerosis patients" (SSc, out of scope; abstract not read). — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29930420/)

### Inferences
- A Chinese prospective study would add value in two ways: from population (MDA5-rich, the RP-ILD burden) and from design (prospective, longitudinal). Existing Chinese work is cross-sectional or retrospective.

### Gaps
- CNKI and Wanfang were not searched, and non-PubMed Chinese literature may contain additional NVC–CTD-ILD reports. This should be checked before claiming novelty in a Chinese grant application.

## 6. Explicit evidence gaps: subgroups with zero longitudinal NVC–ILD outcome data

### Takeaway
Across all non-SSc CTDs, **no prospective study was found that uses baseline or serial NVC to predict ILD progression** (FVC/DLCO decline, HRCT progression, PPF) **or ILD-specific mortality**. The closest are: Wakura 2020 (retrospective; microhaemorrhage vs ILD death in DM-ILD); Xu 2025 (retrospective; NVC cluster, all-cause survival null); and treatment-response studies that track NVC change against activity, not lung outcome. The evidence by subgroup:

| Subgroup | Cross-sectional NVC–ILD association | Longitudinal NVC change data | NVC → ILD progression | NVC → mortality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DM (adult) | Yes but conflicting (Selva-O'Callaghan 2010 +; Sieiro Santos 2025 + for IIM; Mugii 2011 −) | Yes, with activity and treatment (Kubo 2019; Johnson 2021; Miossi 2019; Pizzorni 2018 stable) | **None found** | Xu 2025: no difference (retrospective) |
| ASS (Jo1/non-Jo1) | Non-specific pattern ↔ ILD (Torres-Ruiz 2024); SSc-like ↔ Jo1 (Sebastiani 2019) | Minimal (Mugii 2023 ARS: haemorrhage only) | **None found** | **None found** |
| Anti-MDA5 DM / CADM | Microhaemorrhage/loss ↔ HRCT fibrosis (Wakura 2020); NVC score ↔ FVC (+) (Sugimoto 2022) | Yes, rapid reversal (Sugimoto 2022; Hamaguchi 2021; Mugii 2023) | **None found** (no RP-ILD prediction study) | Microhaemorrhage higher in ILD death (Wakura 2020, retrospective, small) |
| PM / IMNM | NVC mostly normal | — | None | None |
| JDM | Low density ↔ subclinical lung impairment (Barth 2019) | Yes, for activity | None | None |
| IPAF / UCTD | Diagnostic discrimination only (Umashankar 2022; Lee 2023; Jee 2021; Sabella 2026) | — | **None found** (only CTD-evolution signal: Decker 2022 trend; Sambataro 2023 no overall predictor) | **None found** |
| MCTD | Capillary loss/avascular areas ↔ ILD (Kasser 2025; Diógenes 2007) | Yes (Todoroki 2022; Paolino 2019; Diógenes 2007) | **None found** | None (NVC → dCTD evolution, Chevalier 2024) |
| RA | One tiny study (n=7 RA-ILD; Karadoğan 2015) | None | **None found** | **None found** |
| SLE | NVC ↔ DLCO (Groen 1992) | cSLE pattern change (Schonenberg-Meinema 2022) | **None found** | **None found** |
| Sjögren's | One study, density ↔ ILD (per Melsens 2020) | None | **None found** | **None found** |

### Inferences
- The clearest original contribution would be a **prospective, multi-CTD (non-SSc) ILD cohort** with standardised NVC (EULAR/SCTC parameters, quantitative density, avascular areas and microhaemorrhages, with blinded reading) at baseline and serial time points, combined with quantitative HRCT. Endpoints would be FVC/DLCO decline, PPF, RP-ILD and mortality, stratified by MSA (MDA5 vs ARS) and with adjustment for UIP vs NSIP pattern.
- Given its rapid reversibility, NVC in IIM and MCTD may be more informative as **"delta NVC"** (early change after treatment) than as a baseline snapshot. That design has not been tested against lung outcomes.

### Gaps
- Full texts were not reviewed, so secondary ILD analyses buried in the results of the large cohorts (Sebastiani 2019, Torres-Ruiz 2024, Leclair 2026, Chevalier 2024) cannot be excluded.
- Two possibly relevant 2026 longitudinal JDM papers were not fully read: PMID 41764972 (Semin Arthritis Rheum 2026; 1-year NVC biomarker study) and PMID 42220959. Their abstracts appeared to focus on disease activity, not ILD.
- CNKI, Wanfang and Embase were not searched.
