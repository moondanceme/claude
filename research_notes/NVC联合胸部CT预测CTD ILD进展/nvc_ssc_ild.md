# Nailfold videocapillaroscopy (NVC) and interstitial lung disease in systemic sclerosis (SSc-ILD): cross-sectional, longitudinal and prognostic evidence

Search method: PubMed searches through the PubMed MCP, run on 2026-10-28. Queries combined capillaroscopy/nailfold/capillary density/MES/CSURI with SSc and ILD/FVC/DLCO/prognosis/treatment terms (about 270 + 366 + 66 + 101 + 34 hits screened by title). Abstracts were retrieved for every cited study. Full text was read for the SENSCIS NVC substudy (PMC12542725) and the EUSTAR incident-ILD analysis (PMC11701895). The Ickinger 2026 review and Sieiro Santos 2025 are paywalled (HTTP 403 / not open access), so only their abstracts were used. Every study below was retrieved. PMIDs and DOIs are given in the master table.

---

## Q1. Cross-sectional associations of NVC with ILD presence/extent and PFTs (effect sizes)

### Takeaway
Cross-sectional studies agree well with each other. Capillary loss/low density, avascular areas, neoangiogenesis and the "late" (or "active/late") pattern are associated with ILD presence, more extensive HRCT disease and lower FVC/DLCO. Effect sizes are moderate in large registries (OR ≈1.3–1.8 for late pattern vs ILD) and much larger in small single-centre series (OR up to ~18, likely inflated). Two registry analyses reported that the association persisted after adjustment for autoantibodies.

### Cited Findings
- The 2020 systematic review by Smith et al., which applied EULAR standardised definitions, retained only 2 cross-sectional studies and 1 longitudinal study. Both cross-sectional studies found SSc-ILD inversely associated with capillary density (p=0.008 and p=0.005), and one found an association with the active/late pattern — [Smith 2020 Autoimmun Rev 102619, PMID 32663627](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32663627/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autrev.2020.102619))
- Each neoangiogenic capillary/mm corresponds to a 7.3% lower FVC, and each giant capillary/mm to an 11.8% lower DLCO (n=134, 8-image quantitative NVC) — [Guillén-Del-Castillo 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30567570/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13075-018-1775-9))
- The association between NVC pattern and ILD (OR 1.3–1.4) and low DLCO (OR 1.5) was independent of the specific anti-ENA antibody — [Markusse 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27940596/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/kew402))
- Capillary loss OR 18.57 (95% CI 2.17–158.72) for ILD, n=48 — [Caetano 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29782426/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1097/RHU.0000000000000815))
- Late pattern associated with ILD, OR 1.29 in n=1356 — [RESCLE 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37534953/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.55563/clinexprheumatol/8lrofr)); OR 1.83 in n=1689 — [SPRING, Tonutti 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42367240/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtauto.2026.100381))
- Active pattern independently associated with FVC <70% among 595 SSc-ILD patients — [Spanish SSc group 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29322341/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00296-017-3916-x))
- Severe NCM changes associated with ground-glass opacities (a marker of activity) in early disease, but NCM did not predict reduced DLCO — [Bredemeier 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14760798/)
- Density ≤4.78/mm discriminated ILD with AUC 0.815 — [De Angelis 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38929856/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/jpm14060635))
- An SSc pattern was associated with abnormal PFT independent of SSc diagnosis (35.9% vs 19.5%) — [van Roon 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30554151/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3899/jrheum.180615))
- An older review (Soulaidopoulos 2017) concluded that extent of peripheral microangiopathy reflects cardiopulmonary severity but that predictive use "remains to be established" — [Soulaidopoulos 2017 Autoimmun Rev](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28576600/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autrev.2017.05.019))

### Inferences
- The most consistent lung-related NVC parameter is capillary loss (low density, avascular areas). Neoangiogenesis and giant capillaries are linked to FVC and DLCO respectively in one well-designed quantitative study.
- Effect sizes fall as sample size rises: OR ~18 (n=48) → ~4 (n=70) → 1.3–1.8 (n=287–1689). This fits small-study inflation and/or selection of more severe tertiary patients.
- Late pattern prevalence rises with disease duration (SPRING-SIR). Unless analyses adjust for duration, some of the cross-sectional association may reflect duration and cumulative severity rather than a lung-specific link.

### Gaps
- No meta-analytic pooled effect size for NVC vs ILD presence was found among the retrieved papers.
- Few studies relate NVC to quantitative HRCT extent (e.g. Goh staging or % extent). Most use ILD yes/no or PFTs.

## Q2. Longitudinal studies: does baseline NVC or change in NVC predict incident ILD, FVC/DLCO decline, HRCT progression or death?

### Takeaway
The evidence is mixed. Positive results include Sieiro Santos 2025 (n=74, 2 y), the Smith 2012/2013 Medsger-lung composite, SCLEROCAP and Bournia 2020 (composites including FVC/DLCO), and EUSTAR transitions (worsening pattern → annual ILD OR 3.76). Negative or neutral results include Vilela 2022 (n=79, 24 mo; no association with PFT worsening), EUSTAR baseline pattern vs 5-y incident ILD (adjusted HR 1.09, NS), Pavan 2017 (avascular score vs mortality blunted after adjustment for ILD extent) and SENSCIS (descriptive, no testing). The strongest and most reproducible longitudinal signal is change in NVC (progressive capillary loss / pattern worsening), not a single baseline pattern.

### Cited Findings
- **Positive (baseline):** In SSc, baseline capillary disorganisation predicted 2-year ΔFVC (β −0.75) and ΔDLCO (β −0.24), and the late pattern predicted ΔDLCO (β −0.38). HRCT semi-quantitative fibrosis progression was predicted by SCTC-DI, not NVC, according to the abstract — [Sieiro Santos 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40600932/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/keaf361))
- **Negative (baseline):** In the same type of design (n=79, 24 mo, EULAR-standardised), no association was found between baseline NVC and PFT worsening, although the cross-sectional baseline association was present — [Vilela 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34741001/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1097/RHU.0000000000001798))
- **Negative (baseline) / positive (change), large registry:** Baseline pattern did not predict 5-y incident ILD after adjustment (HR 1.09, 0.86–1.39), but annual worsening of pattern did (OR 3.76, 1.99–7.11). Images were not centrally re-read, only qualitative patterns were used, and capillary density was not analysed — [Velauthapillai 2025 EUSTAR](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39777215/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/23971983241307692)); full text [PMC11701895](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11701895/)
- Baseline NVC pattern predicted future severe Medsger lung involvement in a graded way (adjusted OR late 12.68 vs normal) — [Smith 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22402146/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1136/annrheumdis-2011-200780)). The finding was replicated for all-organ outcomes in two cohorts — [Smith 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24128778/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3899/jrheum.130528))
- In the multicentre EUSTAR follow-up (n=334), associations were found for overall progression, but the study was underpowered for organ-specific outcomes such as lung — [Vanhaecke 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35176132/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/keac079))
- Sequential NVC: capillary loss over time predicted overall progression (HR 4.35) and lung vascular progression (HR 18.53) — [Avouac 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28291582/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semarthrit.2017.02.006))
- The Smith 2020 SR identified one longitudinal study in which incident SSc-ILD was associated with progressive capillary loss (p=0.03) and conversion to an active/late pattern (p=0.001/0.003) — [Smith 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32663627/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autrev.2020.102619))
- A 2026 SR restricted to longitudinal, centrally/EULAR-read studies from the last 10 years (14 studies, QUIPS low–moderate risk of bias) concluded that density <7/mm and the active/late pattern "consistently predicted" progression, including ILD — [Ickinger 2026 Best Pract Res Clin Rheumatol](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41826085/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.berh.2026.102130)). Abstract only. Note that this conclusion does not engage the negative EUSTAR baseline result in its abstract.
- The earlier SR (18 studies) found 17/18 positive, but 13/18 had moderate–high risk of bias for confounding and for statistical analysis. It noted that NVC evolution may matter more than baseline — [Paxton & Pauling 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29602558/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semarthrit.2018.02.005))
- Composite outcomes: SCLEROCAP late stage + short duration + age gave AUC 0.74 for severe (cardio-pulmonary-renal) progression — [Boulon 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40967366/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mvr.2025.104874)). Active (OR 3.31) and late (OR 6.90) patterns predicted a 3-y composite that included FVC/DLCO decline — [Bournia 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31969216/)
- Mortality: avascular score >1.5 independently predicted death (HR 2.27; 12 deaths) — [Kayser 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23324736/). In a 10-y cohort (73 deaths), the avascular score HR fell from 1.64 to 1.15 (NS) after adjustment that included ILD extent — [Pavan 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29018976/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10067-017-3869-1)). Baseline avascular areas predicted all-cause mortality (HR 2.09) over 11.7 y — [Semin Arthritis Rheum 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41076942/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semarthrit.2025.152845)). In the Spanish registry, an active pattern was associated with reduced survival only in univariate analysis — [Simeón-Aznar 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26512564/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000001728))
- The SENSCIS placebo-arm prediction model for 52-week ILD progression (FVC decline >5% or death) used DLCO, time since first non-Raynaud symptom, mRSS, ATA and MMF. NVC is not among the reported predictors (AUC 0.63 overall; 0.75 in the early/inflammatory subgroup) — [SENSCIS model 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39242112/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1136/rmdopen-2024-004240))

### Inferences
- Taken together, the evidence suggests NVC is a marker of global disease severity and of "vascular phenotype" more than a lung-specific predictor of FVC decline. Where lung outcomes are isolated (Vilela; EUSTAR baseline Cox; SENSCIS; Mugii after adjustment), signals are null or weak. Where outcomes are composites or Medsger lung categories (Smith, Bournia, SCLEROCAP), signals are strong.
- The DLCO signal (Sieiro Santos, Mugii, Capparelli, Markusse) is more consistent than the FVC signal. This is biologically plausible because DLCO captures pulmonary vasculopathy as well as ILD.
- No study retrieved evaluated NVC against a formal PPF/INBUILD-type definition, or radiological progression measured by quantitative CT.

### Gaps
- The full-text covariate set of Sieiro Santos 2025 could not be verified (paywalled), so it is unclear whether baseline ILD extent, FVC or ATA were adjusted for.
- The specific longitudinal study in the Smith 2020 SR (capillary loss → incident ILD) could not be identified from the abstract.
- Study-level details of the Ickinger 2026 review (paywalled) could not be extracted.
- No study used PPF (ATS/ERS/JRS/ALAT 2022) as an outcome.

## Q3. Is NVC's prognostic value independent of baseline ILD extent, FVC and autoantibodies?

### Takeaway
Only a few studies adjusted for the key confounders. When baseline FVC/DLCO and ATA/anti-RNAPIII were included (EUSTAR 2025), baseline NVC pattern lost significance for incident ILD (HR 1.32 → 1.09), while pattern transitions remained significant. When ILD extent was included for mortality (Pavan 2017), the avascular score lost significance. Cross-sectional associations with ILD did survive autoantibody adjustment (Markusse 2017, Trefler 2026). Many positive longitudinal studies adjusted only for duration, subset or medication (Smith 2012/2013) or did not report their covariates in the abstract.

### Cited Findings
- EUSTAR Cox model adjusted for sex, dcSSc, ethnicity, ATA, anti-RNAPIII, age, FVC% and DLCO%. Baseline pattern HR went from 1.32 (unadjusted, significant) to 1.09 (adjusted, NS). ACA was not retained in the final model — [Velauthapillai 2025, PMC11701895](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11701895/)
- After adjustment for age, skin score, sex, race, ischaemia and ILD extent, the avascular score–mortality HR fell to 1.15 (NS) — [Pavan 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29018976/)
- NVC–ILD association independent of anti-ENA subtype (ACA, ATA, RNAPIII, RNP) — [Markusse 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27940596/). About 4-fold odds of ILD for pattern grade 4–5 after adjustment for anti-Scl-70, duration or mRSS — [Trefler 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42063790/)
- Smith 2012/2013 adjusted only for disease duration, subset and vasoactive medication — [Smith 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22402146/); [Smith 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24128778/)
- ATA/nucleolar ANA are associated with faster NVC progression — [Sulli 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31750929/). ATA is associated with the late pattern — [SPRING-SIR 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41392303/); [RESCLE 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37534953/). ACA is associated with early pattern and ATA with active/late in 8/13 studies — [Hysa 2023 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36648887/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/antib12010003))
- The paramount confounding problem was flagged in the SR: 13/18 studies had moderate–high risk of confounder bias — [Paxton & Pauling 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29602558/)
- Baseline DLCO and ATA (not NVC) drive the SENSCIS progression model — [SENSCIS model 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39242112/)

### Inferences
- ATA and dcSSc lie on the causal and confounding path: they are associated with both a worse NVC pattern and ILD. Without adjustment, NVC may act partly as a proxy for ATA/dcSSc.
- Change in NVC (transitions) may add information beyond baseline FVC and antibodies, based on one large registry. This is the most promising claim of incremental value, but it still needs replication with central reading and quantitative density.

### Gaps
- No study retrieved reports formal incremental value (ΔC-index, NRI) of NVC on top of a baseline HRCT extent + FVC + ATA model for ILD progression.
- Whether NVC predicts progression within ATA-positive or within ACA-positive strata is unreported.

## Q4. Does NVC change over time and with treatment? Is NVC change related to lung outcomes?

### Takeaway
NVC changes in roughly half of patients over 3 years and in about three quarters over 12 years. Change can go in both directions (improvement in ~23% over 10 y). Treatment-related change has been reported with bosentan + iloprost (density recovery), tocilizumab (density rise correlating with PFT improvement, n=13) and possibly nintedanib (numerical, untested). Methylprednisolone pulses showed no effect, and nintedanib had no overall effect in SENSCIS. Change in NVC relates to lung outcomes in EUSTAR (incident ILD) and Avouac (lung vascular progression). Mugii (DLCO) lost significance after adjustment.

### Cited Findings
- 51% of patients had significant NVC changes over 3 y. Capillary loss over time predicted progression — [Avouac 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28291582/)
- 76% of patients in the "early" pattern changed over 12 y — [Sulli 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31750929/). About 81% kept the same pattern in the first 3 y in EUSTAR, and 1 patient moved late→early — [Velauthapillai 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11701895/)
- Over 10 y, NVC improved in 19 patients, was unchanged in 41 and worsened in 24 (n=84). The worsened group had the greatest ΔDLCO decline (p=0.0067), which was NS after adjustment for ILD/PH (p=0.068). ΔVC did not differ — [Mugii 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42922009/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1346-8138.70531))
- Nintedanib (SENSCIS NVC substudy, 52 wk, 8 fingers, central reader): no overall change. Post hoc, density was stable on nintedanib vs a numerical fall on placebo in the risk-factor subgroup. Analyses were descriptive only, and many images were non-evaluable — [SENSCIS NVC 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41107055/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1136/rmdopen-2025-005704)); full text [PMC12542725](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12542725/)
- Bosentan + iloprost over 4 y (n=30): progressive increase in absolute capillary number and fingertip perfusion in the combination group, with DLCO and sPAP not worsening — [Trombetta 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27744392/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3899/jrheum.160592))
- Iloprost ± bosentan over 1 y (n=95): fewer giant capillaries in both groups. CSURI worsened significantly on iloprost alone and was stable with the combination — [Cestelli 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27310203/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/14397595.2016.1192761))
- Tocilizumab (n=13): capillary density increased at 6 mo, and the increase correlated positively with PFT improvement — [Segawa 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41664492/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/exd.70220))
- IV methylprednisolone in very early SSc (RCT, n=30): no difference in capillary density at 12 wk (−0.5, 95% CI −1.1 to 0.2) — [Kersten 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38552324/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/keae156))
- CYC/MMF exposure is cross-sectionally associated with worse NVC, interpreted as confounding by severity rather than a treatment effect — [Trefler 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42063790/)

### Inferences
- Signals of NVC response to treatment come from small, mostly uncontrolled studies. Only the SENSCIS substudy and the methylprednisolone RCT are randomised, and neither showed a significant effect.
- Because NVC can improve, a single baseline NVC may misclassify long-term vascular risk. This favours serial NVC in prognostic models.

### Gaps
- No retrieved study tested whether MMF- or CYC-induced change in NVC tracks FVC response.
- No study related MES (Microangiopathy Evolution Score) change specifically to ILD outcomes. MES-lung data were not found in this search.

## Q5. Methodological reasons for contradictory findings

### Takeaway
The contradictions are best explained by:
- the type of exposure (baseline vs change)
- outcome definition (lung-specific FVC decline vs Medsger/composite)
- confounder sets (with or without baseline FVC/DLCO/ATA/ILD extent)
- reading quality (local vs central, qualitative vs quantitative, number of fingers/images, device heterogeneity)
- short follow-up relative to the slow, non-linear course of FVC
- small samples with few events
- spectrum differences: early, ILD-free SSc vs established SSc-ILD in trials, and disease-duration confounding of the late pattern

### Cited Findings
- **Exposure type:** In the same EUSTAR cohort, baseline pattern was NS while transitions were strongly significant — [Velauthapillai 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11701895/). The SR suggests evolution matters more than baseline — [Paxton & Pauling 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29602558/)
- **Outcome definition:** Positive studies used Medsger DSS lung category or composites — [Smith 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22402146/); [Bournia 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31969216/); [Boulon 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40967366/). The negative Vilela study used strict PFT worsening thresholds — [Vilela 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34741001/). SENSCIS used FVC decline >5%/death in only 11 progressors — [SENSCIS NVC 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12542725/)
- **Confounder adjustment:** Including FVC/DLCO/ATA abolished the baseline effect (EUSTAR). Including ILD extent abolished the mortality effect — [Pavan 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29018976/)
- **Reading and standardisation:** EUSTAR had no central re-read and no density analysis — [Velauthapillai 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11701895/). SENSCIS had a single reader, multiple devices and many non-evaluable images — [PMC12542725](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12542725/). Quantitative NVC is unreliable unless the same capillaries/whole nailbed are imaged (ICC 0.90–0.98 for whole mosaics vs poorer for cropped images) — [Murray 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22438458/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/rheumatology/kes007)). Consensus standardisation (EULAR Study Group): image acquisition and 8-finger assessment — [Smith 2020 Autoimmun Rev 102458](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31927087/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autrev.2020.102458)). Single-finger density (non-dominant 4th finger) was the one associated with mortality — [Gheorghiu 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32905140/). The Ickinger 2026 SR restricted inclusion to EULAR-framework longitudinal studies and reached positive conclusions — [Ickinger 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41826085/)
- **Classification systems differ:** Maricq "active" ≈ Cutolo "late". Only two patterns appear clinically useful (giant capillaries/haemorrhages vs severe loss) — [Boulon 2019 SCLEROCAP](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31172926/)
- **Opposite-direction parameters:** Microhaemorrhages and giant capillaries are "protective" for some outcomes (DU HR 0.53 — Avouac; PH OR 0.47 — Vanhaecke; mortality HR 0.35 — Semin Arthritis Rheum 2025). Composite pattern scores therefore mix markers of active remodelling with markers of loss — [Avouac 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28291582/); [Vanhaecke 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35176132/); [PMID 41076942](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41076942/)
- **Disease-duration confounding:** Late pattern 13.2% at ≤5 y vs 36.0% at >10 y. Patterns are "not strictly time-dependent" — [SPRING-SIR 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41392303/)
- **Population spectrum:** EUSTAR included only ILD-free patients with ≤5 y duration who already had a scleroderma pattern (restricted variance; authors say this may underestimate the association). The earlier Vanhaecke EUSTAR study had longer duration and high baseline ILD prevalence — [Velauthapillai 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11701895/)
- **Power:** Vanhaecke states the study was underpowered for infrequent organ outcomes — [Vanhaecke 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35176132/). Kayser had 12 deaths — [Kayser 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23324736/). The Caetano 2019 OR CI spans 2–159 — [Caetano 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29782426/)
- **Specificity:** NVC abnormalities also occur in IPF and non-CTD ILD (reduced density and neoangiogenesis vs COPD), so lung fibrosis itself may affect nailfold microvasculature (reverse causation) — [Corrado 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20547086/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbspin.2010.02.019)); [Clin Rheumatol 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38963466/) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10067-024-07049-5))

### Inferences
- Likely ranking of contradiction drivers:
  1. Baseline vs change exposure
  2. Adjustment for baseline lung function, antibodies or ILD extent
  3. Composite vs lung-specific outcomes
  4. Qualitative local reading vs quantitative central reading with 8 fingers
  5. Follow-up ≤2 y, during which FVC change is small and noisy
- A study design that could resolve the contradictions would use central quantitative 8-finger density with serial annual NVC, a lung-specific outcome (FVC decline ≥5–10%, HRCT extent progression, PPF), adjustment for baseline HRCT extent, FVC, DLCO, ATA, ACA and duration, and incremental-value statistics.

### Gaps
- No individual-patient meta-analysis of NVC and SSc-ILD progression exists among the retrieved papers.
- Inter-reader reliability data specific to the multicentre lung-outcome studies were not reported in the abstracts.
- MES and CSURI have not been tested for ILD outcomes in the retrieved literature. CSURI was developed and used for digital ulcers and treatment monitoring ([Cestelli 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27310203/)).
