# 机制与病理生理：微血管（内皮）损伤与CTD-ILD肺纤维化，以及甲襞微循环是否反映肺微血管损伤

> Scope note: all citations below were retrieved via PubMed in this session (abstract-level unless stated). PMID + DOI recorded for each. "Abstract-only" means full text was not read; numbers come from the abstract. Animal/in-vitro evidence is flagged **[ANIMAL]** / **[IN VITRO]**. Search budget was limited (~20 tool calls), so absence of a citation for a point is noted under Gaps rather than filled from memory.

## Q1. Does endothelial/microvascular injury precede or drive fibrosis in SSc / SSc-ILD (EndMT, pericyte-to-myofibroblast, rarefaction, VEGF, ET-1)? Human vs animal evidence

### Takeaway
The "vasculopathy first" model of SSc is widely held (EULAR microcirculation group systematic review calls SSc "primarily a vascular disease"), and human lung tissue shows EndMT-like cells in SSc-ILD. But direct human evidence that endothelial injury *causes* lung fibrosis, as opposed to coexisting with it, is thin: the human EndMT data come from a few explanted end-stage lungs (n=2–6). Most causal and interventional evidence is from mice (bleomycin, Fra-2 transgenic, CXCL4 knockout) or in vitro. Human single-cell atlases of SSc-ILD point to myofibroblasts and SPP1+ macrophages as the main fibrotic effectors, with vascular programmes most prominent in SSc-PAH.

### Cited Findings
**Paradigm / reviews (human-derived synthesis)**
- EULAR Study Group on Microcirculation systematic review (193 articles): the most reliable EC biomarkers in SSc are adhesion molecules (EC activation), anti-endothelial cell antibodies (EC apoptosis), VEGF/VEGFR-2/endostatin (disturbed angiogenesis), endothelial progenitor cells (defective vasculogenesis), ET-1 (vascular tone), vWF (coagulopathy) and IL-33. Myofibroblasts can derive from ECs via EndMT. The authors conclude "SSc is primarily a vascular disease". Systematic review. PMID 28572048; DOI 10.1016/j.autrev.2017.05.024 — [Autoimmun Rev 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28572048/)
- SSc microangiopathy is reduced capillary density with chaotic architecture, which causes chronic tissue hypoxia, yet compensatory angiogenesis is insufficient and vasculogenesis is impaired. The proposed explanation is an imbalance of angiogenic (VEGF, PDGF, TGF-β, FGF-2, Ang-1, SDF-1, ET-1…) versus angiostatic factors. Narrative review. PMID 21549861; DOI 10.1016/j.autrev.2011.04.019 — [Autoimmun Rev 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21549861/)
- Nat Rev Rheumatol review: fibrosis initiation in SSc involves endothelium and pericyte activation, together with aberrant immunity, ER stress and chronic tissue injury. Vascular injury is one of several initiators here, not the only one. Review. PMID 24752182; DOI 10.1038/nrrheum.2014.53 — [Nat Rev Rheumatol 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24752182/)
- 2021 pathogenesis review: SSc combines immune-inflammatory dysregulation, abnormal endothelial behaviour and trans-differentiation into myofibroblasts. Pericytes, platelets and keratinocytes are listed as additional pathogenic cells. Review. PMID 34487318; DOI 10.1007/s12016-021-08889-8 — [Clin Rev Allergy Immunol 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34487318/)

**EndMT: human lung tissue**
- SSc-ILD lungs (histology n=6; isolated CD31+CD102+ lung ECs from 2 SSc-ILD and 2 controls) showed cells coexpressing vWF and α-SMA in small/medium arterioles. These cells were absent in control lungs. SSc lung ECs expressed COL1, COL3, fibronectin, TGF-β1, CTGF, TWIST1 and SNAI2 alongside EC genes. The authors conclude this demonstrates EndMT in SSc-ILD lungs. **Very small n; explanted end-stage lungs; cross-sectional, so it cannot establish temporal precedence.** PMID 26360820; DOI 10.1002/art.39421 — [Arthritis Rheumatol 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26360820/)
- The same group found that highly purified lung MVECs from end-stage SSc-ILD (n=2 vs 2 controls) overexpress interferon-regulated and antiviral genes (IFI44, ISG15, MX1…) as well as mesenchymal genes. Microarray. PMID 33253099; DOI 10.55563/clinexprheumatol/ret1kg — [Clin Exp Rheumatol 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33253099/)
- A review titled "EndoMT in SSc-associated pulmonary fibrosis and PAH: myth or reality?" states explicitly that the contribution of EndoMT to SSc-ILD/PAH "has not been generally accepted". Review. PMID 26807760; DOI 10.1016/j.matbio.2016.01.012 — [Matrix Biol 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26807760/)
- Single-cell RNA-seq of explanted lungs in SSc-associated PH (n=16), IPAH (n=3) and controls (n=15): two angiogenic EC subtypes (tip and phalanx ECs) were expanded, and "transitional cells" expressing both EC and pericyte/SMC markers were taken as evidence of EndMT. VEGF-A, TGF-β1, angiotensin and TWEAK were implicated. **Note: this is a PH cohort, not an ILD-focused one.** PMID 38841857; DOI 10.1161/ATVBAHA.123.320005 — [ATVB 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38841857/)

**EndMT / vascular-targeted interventions: animal and in vitro**
- **[ANIMAL + IN VITRO]** CXCL4 is raised in SSc and correlates with skin and lung fibrosis. CXCL4-deficient mice are protected from fibrosis, and human CXCL4 overexpression aggravates bleomycin fibrosis. In vitro, CXCL4 induces EndMT and myofibroblast differentiation in ECs. PMID 34986347; DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2021.110189 — [Cell Rep 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34986347/)
- **[ANIMAL]** The DPP4 inhibitor linagliptin reduced bleomycin-induced lung fibrosis and EndMT via Akt/mTOR in an "SSc mouse model" (bleomycin). PMID 34988855; DOI 10.1007/s11010-021-04349-1 — [Mol Cell Biochem 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34988855/)
- **[ANIMAL]** Alpha-ketoglutarate attenuated bleomycin skin and lung fibrosis. scRNA-seq identified ECs, not fibroblasts, as its main cellular target, and treatment restored EC proportions, angiogenic function and barrier integrity. This supports the idea that rescuing endothelium reduces fibrosis, but only in mice. PMID 42892459; DOI 10.1016/j.phrs.2026.109686 — [Pharmacol Res 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42892459/)
- **[ANIMAL]** Fra-2 transgenic mice develop severe pulmonary arteriolar remodelling plus NSIP-like lung disease, "closely resembling human SSc-associated PH". This is the main model in which vascular remodelling and ILD co-develop. PMID 37700377; DOI 10.1186/s13075-023-03143-2 — [Arthritis Res Ther 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37700377/)
- **[ANIMAL]** In Fra-2 TG mice, the lung phenotype (fibrosis plus vascular remodelling) is driven by an IL-2-related Treg deficiency preceding Th2 activation. Low-dose IL-2/Treg restoration was tested. This points to an immune upstream driver in this model, not a purely vascular one. PMID 35255201; DOI 10.1002/art.42111 — [Arthritis Rheumatol 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35255201/)
- **[ANIMAL]** Pirfenidone attenuated bleomycin fibrosis but *aggravated* inflammation, fibrosis and vascular remodelling in Fra-2 TG mice through a Th2/eosinophil response. This shows that model choice changes conclusions about the vascular–fibrotic link. PMID 35332068; DOI 10.1183/13993003.02347-2021 — [Eur Respir J 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35332068/)

**Pericyte-to-myofibroblast**
- Pericytes in SSc share markers with myofibroblasts (ADAM-12, α-SMA, ED-A fibronectin, Thy-1). After PDGF-β or TGF-β stimulation they may acquire a myofibroblast phenotype. The review title stresses "certainties and uncertainties": pericytes are hard to phenotype, and evidence is largely from skin and in vitro. Review. PMID 35382121; DOI 10.5301/jsrd.5000254 — [J Scleroderma Relat Disord 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35382121/)
- **[IN VITRO + human skin]** TGF-β1 (but not TNFα) drives integrin-dependent pericyte migration away from the vessel wall, which correlates with EC angiopoietin-2 (vascular instability). Vascular-stability changes tracked disease state in human SSc **skin**; lung was not studied. PMID 38548268; DOI 10.1016/j.ajpath.2024.02.021 — [Am J Pathol 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38548268/)

**Human single-cell data on fibrotic effectors (counterweight)**
- scRNA-seq of explanted lungs (4 SSc-ILD, 4 controls; 56,196 cells) found a new, large, proliferating myofibroblast population. Myofibroblasts showed the greatest phenotypic changes, and fibroblasts were framed as the key effectors. PMID 31405848; DOI 10.1136/annrheumdis-2018-214865 — [Ann Rheum Dis 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31405848/)
- Multiomic snATAC/RNA-seq of SSc-ILD lungs: fibroblasts showed AP-1/RUNX/EGR-driven profibrotic programmes (CTHRC1, ADAM12) and macrophages showed SPP1/CCL18 programmes. Endothelium was not highlighted as a driver. PMID 41478761; DOI 10.1016/j.ard.2025.12.003 — [Ann Rheum Dis 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41478761/)
- Systematic review of single-cell studies in SSc (17 studies, 358 patients; 9 SSc-ILD lung studies): immune abnormality dominates in PBMCs, **fibrosis in SSc-ILD lung**, and **vasculopathy in SSc-PAH**. Systematic review. PMID 38818750; DOI 10.1080/25785826.2024.2360690 — [Immunol Med 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38818750/)

### Inferences
- The evidence supports the claim that endothelial injury is *part of* SSc lung pathobiology (EndMT-like cells, IFN-activated ECs, angiogenic imbalance). It does not establish temporal precedence or causality for ILD in humans. Causal data are animal-only, and the leading SSc-ILD animal model (Fra-2) is immune-driven upstream.
- For grant wording, "microvascular injury is a plausible upstream/parallel driver" is defensible. "Proven upstream driver of lung fibrosis" is not.
- The single-cell systematic review assigns vasculopathy mainly to PAH and fibroblast/macrophage programmes to ILD. This argues that nailfold changes may track the pulmonary *vascular* compartment (DLCO, PH) more directly than the *fibrotic* compartment.

### Gaps
- No human longitudinal study was found showing that lung microvascular loss precedes ILD onset. Nailfold capillary loss preceding ILD has only indirect support (see Q3).
- ET-1 and VEGF causal roles in human SSc-ILD (e.g., results of ERA trials in SSc-ILD, such as bosentan BUILD-2) were not retrieved this session; they are not cited here. (My understanding is that ERA trials did not show ILD benefit, but this is unverified in this session.)
- No retrieved paper quantifies endothelial progenitor cells or circulating endothelial cells against ILD progression.

## Q2. Histological / imaging evidence of lung capillary loss or microvascular change in SSc-ILD / CTD-ILD

### Takeaway
I did not retrieve a study quantifying alveolar capillary density in SSc-ILD lung biopsies. Human lung-tissue vascular evidence is limited to small explant studies: EndMT cells in arterioles, IFN-activated MVECs, and expanded angiogenic ECs in SSc-PH. Quantitative CT shows *increased* (not decreased) arterial vessel number and volume in SSc-ILD, which correlates with ILD extent and lung function. That finding is hard to read as capillary rarefaction, because CT resolves only vessels of roughly 1 mm or more.

### Cited Findings
- Quantitative CT vessel analysis in 79 SSc patients (40 with ILD): pulmonary vascular volume parameters correlated with PFTs and ILD pattern extent. SSc-ILD patients had **increased** number and volume of arterial vessels (2–4 mm) and higher density of arteries/veins <6 mm. The authors propose CT vessel metrics as severity biomarkers. Retrospective cross-sectional, with descriptive longitudinal trends. PMID 35708639; DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keac311 — [Rheumatology 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35708639/)
- In 31 SSc-ILD patients with serial CT, quantitative (CALIPER) and semi-quantitative scores correlated with pulmonary artery diameter. A PA diameter ≥29 mm identified ILD extent >39%. Changes in QA patterns did not predict functional progression (AUC 0.50–0.70, NS). Retrospective. PMID 30861018; DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0213444 — [PLoS One 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30861018/)
- Human lung-tissue vascular evidence (detail in Q1): EndMT-type cells in SSc-ILD arterioles (n=6) PMID 26360820 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26360820/); IFN-signature MVECs (n=2) PMID 33253099 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33253099/); angiogenic tip/phalanx ECs expanded in SSc-PH (n=16) PMID 38841857 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38841857/).
- scRNA-seq in SSc-PAH vs IPAH (4 vs 4 vs 4 donors): SSc-PAH lungs had more fibroblasts and DC/macrophages than IPAH. This suggests the vascular disease in SSc is intertwined with fibroblast expansion. PMID 40686216; DOI 10.1161/CIRCGEN.124.004936 — [Circ Genom Precis Med 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40686216/)

### Inferences
- The CT data run *opposite* to a simple "capillary rarefaction mirrors nailfold capillary loss" story at the macro-vessel level. Larger arterial volume in ILD may reflect traction, redistribution, or vessel remodelling. A grant should not claim CT shows lung capillary loss in SSc-ILD without a specific source.
- DLCO is affected by both the membrane (fibrosis) and capillary blood volume. Without partitioning (DLNO/DLCO, KCO, Vc), a DLCO–NVC association cannot isolate the vascular component.

### Gaps
- No retrieved study of alveolar capillary density or septal capillary morphology in SSc-ILD, IIM-ILD or RA-ILD biopsies. A targeted search on "systemic sclerosis lung capillary density histology" returned only nailfold papers.
- No retrieved study on KCO or DLNO/DLCO (membrane vs capillary blood volume) in SSc-ILD in relation to NVC. This is a key missing link.
- No retrieved study of CT perfusion, dual-energy CT iodine maps or small-vessel (<5 mm²) metrics in CTD-ILD related to NVC.

## Q3. Do nailfold capillary changes correlate with lung vascular/functional status (DLCO, PH, circulating endothelial markers) and predict ILD?

### Takeaway
There is consistent cross-sectional evidence in SSc that capillary loss, avascular areas and late or active patterns associate with ILD presence, lower FVC and lower DLCO, and with PAH. Longitudinal evidence is mixed. Some prospective cohorts show baseline NVC predicting later severe lung involvement or FVC/DLCO decline (OR rising across early→active→late patterns; capillary disorganization predicting ΔFVC/ΔDLCO). Others find no association with PFT worsening at 24 months. A 2026 systematic review limited to longitudinal, centrally read studies concludes that low density (<7/mm) and active/late patterns predict ILD and PAH. **None of the retrieved studies tested whether NVC adds prognostic value beyond baseline HRCT fibrosis extent.** That is exactly the gap the grant would fill, and also its main vulnerability.

### Cited Findings
**Cross-sectional SSc**
- In 134 SSc patients, those with ILD had lower capillary density (4.86 vs 5.88/mm, p=0.005) and more neoangiogenesis (0.56 vs 0.31/mm). Each neoangiogenic capillary/mm was associated with a 7.3% lower FVC, and each giant capillary/mm with an 11.8% lower DLCO. PAH was associated with more neoangiogenesis. Authors: "a clue for vasculopathy as a pathogenic mechanism for lung injury". Cross-sectional. PMID 30567570; DOI 10.1186/s13075-018-1775-9 — [Arthritis Res Ther 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30567570/)
- In 48 SSc patients (21 with ILD), capillary loss gave OR 18.57 for ILD (95% CI 2.17–158.7; AUC 0.90) and avascular areas gave OR 4.64. Avascular areas were associated with FVC −18.1% and DLCO −14.0%. Cross-sectional; wide CIs. PMID 29782426; DOI 10.1097/RHU.0000000000000815 — [J Clin Rheumatol 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29782426/)
- In 85 SSc patients, those with density loss had worse FVC (86.9 vs 101.1%) and DLCO (71.4 vs 85.9%), both p<0.01. Retrospective. PMID 25399393; DOI 10.3899/jrheum.140276 — [J Rheumatol 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25399393/)
- Spanish registry (1,374 SSc; 595 with ILD): an *active* NVC pattern was independently associated with FVC <70%, alongside dcSSc, anti-Scl-70, ground glass and low DLCO. Registry, cross-sectional. PMID 29322341; DOI 10.1007/s00296-017-3916-x — [Rheumatol Int 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29322341/)
- Comparing 23 SSc-PF, 20 IPF and 22 COPD patients: IPF showed minor NVC changes (lower density and more neoangiogenesis than COPD), while SSc-PF had typical scleroderma changes. This suggests some systemic microvascular change even in IPF, so the signal is not unique to CTD. PMID 20547086; DOI 10.1016/j.jbspin.2010.02.019 — [Joint Bone Spine 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20547086/)

**PH / pulmonary vascular**
- In SSc-PAH (n=19) vs SSc-noPAH (n=20), active/late patterns were more frequent (73.2 vs 50%), with lower density (5.64 vs 6.5/mm) and more neoangiogenesis. IPAH (n=21) also showed reduced density (7.5 vs 9/mm in controls). The authors note it is unknown whether systemic microcirculatory change is causal or consequential. PMID 28619664; DOI 10.1016/j.mvr.2017.06.005 — [Microvasc Res 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28619664/)
- In SLE, a scleroderma NVC pattern is among the risk factors for SLE-PAH, alongside DLCO <70% and ILD. Systematic review (21 studies) plus Delphi. PMID 37794618; DOI 10.1002/acr2.11611 — [ACR Open Rheumatol 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37794618/)
- In 230 SSc patients, the renal resistive index was higher with active/late NVC patterns. ΔRRI predicted new PAH (AUC 0.84). This supports NVC as part of a systemic vasculopathy phenotype. Retrospective. PMID 32238514; DOI 10.3899/jrheum.191101 — [J Rheumatol 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32238514/)

**Longitudinal / prognostic**
- Pilot study (66 SSc): NVC patterns predicted future severe *lung* involvement (Medsger DSS) at 18–24 months. Multivariable OR (adjusted for disease duration, subset and vasoactive drugs) was 2.33 for early, 5.44 for active and 12.68 for late patterns vs normal. Prospective. PMID 22402146; DOI 10.1136/annrheumdis-2011-200780 — [Ann Rheum Dis 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22402146/)
- Validation in two cohorts (66 Belgian, 82 Italian): adjusted OR for novel future severe organ involvement (any of 9 organs) was 2.99/1.88 for early, 8.93/3.54 for active and 26.69/6.66 for late patterns. This outcome is not lung-specific. PMID 24128778; DOI 10.3899/jrheum.130528 — [J Rheumatol 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24128778/)
- Prospective study (74 SSc, 2 years): baseline capillary disorganization predicted ΔFVC (β −0.75) and ΔDLCO (β −0.24). A late pattern predicted ΔDLCO (β −0.38) and rising KL-6. ILD prevalence rose from 38% to 51%. **But the baseline SCTC-DI predicted HRCT fibrosis progression, and the abstract does not report whether NVC kept significance after adjusting for baseline HRCT extent.** PMID 40600932; DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keaf361 — [Rheumatology 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40600932/)
- **Negative longitudinal study.** In 79 Brazilian SSc patients followed for 24 months, reduced density was associated with low *baseline* FVC (OR 11) and the late pattern with low *baseline* DLCO (OR 6.75). There was **no association between NVC and PFT worsening over time**. PMID 34741001; DOI 10.1097/RHU.0000000000001798 — [J Clin Rheumatol 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34741001/)
- Systematic review (14 longitudinal studies, last 10 years, central EULAR-standard reading, QUIPS low–moderate RoB): capillary density <7/mm and active/late patterns consistently predicted progression and were associated with ILD, PAH, DU and skin fibrosis. Reduced density "reflects a globally more severe phenotype". PMID 41826085; DOI 10.1016/j.berh.2026.102130 — [Best Pract Res Clin Rheumatol 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41826085/)
- SENSCIS sub-study (SSc-ILD RCT, NVC at baseline and week 52): no overall NVC change with nintedanib or placebo. In patients with risk factors for rapid FVC decline (n=38), density fell numerically on placebo and was stable on nintedanib. Among progressors (n=11), density rose numerically on nintedanib. **Only numerical differences; very small subgroups.** PMID 41107055; DOI 10.1136/rmdopen-2025-005704 — [RMD Open 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41107055/)
- Tocilizumab in 13 SSc patients: capillary density increased at 6 months, and this correlated strongly with PFT improvement. Falls in serum VEGF-A tracked PFT improvement, and HGF correlated best with capillary density. Small uncontrolled series. It suggests NVC is *dynamic* and co-varies with lung function under treatment. PMID 41664492; DOI 10.1111/exd.70220 — [Exp Dermatol 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41664492/)
- KL-6 (an epithelial injury marker) predicted DLCO decline in two SSc cohorts (n=189 retrospective, n=118 prospective), independent of severity. This is relevant as a competing "epithelial" prognostic marker. PMID 33336433; DOI 10.1111/resp.13988 — [Respirology 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33336433/)

### Inferences
- The best-supported statement is "NVC abnormality associates with ILD presence and severity and with PAH, and in several prospective cohorts predicts future lung involvement or decline." Whether NVC is *independent of current fibrotic burden* is untested in the retrieved literature. Several cross-sectional associations (e.g., 34741001) suggest NVC may simply track existing damage.
- The NVC–DLCO associations, especially with giant capillaries and late pattern, may reflect the vascular component of DLCO or concurrent PH. Studies seldom adjust for PH or HRCT extent, so confounding by overall disease severity and subset (dcSSc, anti-Scl-70) is likely.
- The tocilizumab and SENSCIS data suggest NVC density is modifiable and moves with lung function. This supports a "shared active process" more than a fixed marker of damage, but it rests on tiny samples.

### Gaps
- No retrieved study directly correlates NVC with serum ET-1, vWF, EPCs or CECs together with lung outcomes. The search "nailfold videocapillaroscopy serum VEGF endothelin-1 SSc correlation" returned 0 hits. The tocilizumab study (VEGF-A, HGF, PECAM-1) is the only retrieved one.
- No retrieved study adjusts NVC for quantitative HRCT fibrosis extent when predicting progression (incremental C-index or NRI). This is the main evidentiary gap.
- The full text of PMID 40600932 was not read. Its covariate set (whether it includes baseline HRCT extent) is unknown.

## Q4. Does the vascular-driver paradigm extend to IIM (DM, ASS, anti-MDA5), MCTD, and not to RA, SLE or Sjögren's ILD?

### Takeaway
The vasculopathy link is strongest in SSc. It is reasonably supported in dermatomyositis (including anti-MDA5): scleroderma-like patterns are common in DM, and in DM-ILD microhaemorrhages and capillary loss correlate with HRCT fibrosis score and ILD death. It is weaker in anti-synthetase syndrome, where non-specific patterns dominate and Jo-1 associates with non-specific changes. Evidence in MCTD is sparse. In RA, SLE and Sjögren's ILD, the retrieved studies are tiny and inconsistent; they show reduced density in some ILD subgroups but no mechanistic basis. A defensible primary population for the hypothesis is SSc plus DM/MDA5, possibly MCTD with scleroderma features. RA-ILD and Sjögren's ILD should be treated as exploratory.

### Cited Findings
**IIM**
- DM-ILD (Japan), anti-MDA5 vs anti-ARS: microhaemorrhage and disorganization scores were higher with anti-MDA5, while tortuosity was higher with anti-ARS. Microhaemorrhage was higher in ILD-related deaths (p=0.02). Microhaemorrhage and capillary loss correlated with the total HRCT fibrosis score. PMID 32973255; DOI 10.1038/s41598-020-72752-7 — [Sci Rep 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32973255/)
- In 76 DM patients (compared with 26 ASS and 33 SSc), 73.7% had abnormal NVC (reduced density, abnormal morphology). Changes were milder than in SSc but more prominent than in ASS. An NVC-defined cluster was more likely to develop ILD, but survival did not differ. PMID 39689020; DOI 10.1093/rheumatology/keae677 — [Rheumatology 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39689020/)
- In 90 IIM patients, a scleroderma pattern was seen in 81.9% of DM and 76.9% of ADM, versus mostly non-specific changes in PM. The scleroderma pattern correlated with ILD. Anti-Jo1 was more frequent with non-specific changes. Cross-sectional. PMID 39337037; DOI 10.3390/jcm13185550 — [J Clin Med 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39337037/)
- In a multi-ethnic IIM cohort (n=155), the non-specific pattern (53.9%) was associated with **ILD, Jo-1 and ASS**, whereas the SSc pattern was associated with DM/overlap and TIF1-γ. After treatment, capillary density rose and avascular areas fell (n=23 with follow-up). PMID 38488092; DOI 10.55563/clinexprheumatol/l9gudh — [Clin Exp Rheumatol 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38488092/)
- **Implication:** in ASS, ILD occurs with non-specific (milder) microangiopathy. This weakens "NVC severity tracks lung fibrosis" for ASS.
- Juvenile CTD (n=50; JSSc, JDM, JSLE): a higher avascular score was found with ILD, and density ≤4/mm was associated with ILD (5/10 vs 4/13). PMID 41487110; DOI 10.55563/clinexprheumatol/6bz7ok — [Clin Exp Rheumatol 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41487110/)

**MCTD / overlap**
- Small series (n=15: 8 MCTD, 7 DM): scleroderma-like microangiopathy in 47%, with the early pattern in MCTD and the late pattern in DM. Too small for ILD inference. PMID 39027693; DOI 10.5114/ada.2024.141158 — [Postepy Dermatol Alergol 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39027693/)
- SSc-overlap syndromes (n=70) vs lcSSc (n=65): overlap had more non-specific NVC patterns and fewer scleroderma patterns, yet similar lung involvement and PAH. No relationship was found between NVC pattern and organ involvement in the overlap group. **This is evidence of dissociation.** PMID 38322100; DOI 10.5114/reum/175508 — [Reumatologia 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38322100/)

**RA / Sjögren's / SLE**
- 15 CTD-ILD (8 Sjögren's ILD, 7 RA-ILD) vs 18 idiopathic ILD vs 17 controls, none with Raynaud's: capillary density was reduced in CTD-ILD. RA-ILD, Sjögren's ILD and IPF had more abnormalities, and density was lower in UIP than in NSIP. Very small. PMID 25849052; DOI 10.5578/tt.8673 — [Tuberk Toraks 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25849052/)
- Sjögren's systematic review (7 quality-retained studies): only one study linked capillary density with ILD. Evidence is weak. PMID 33025880 — [Clin Exp Rheumatol 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33025880/) (DOI not provided in PubMed record)
- Narrative review of NVC in CTD-ILD: low density is associated with ILD presence and severity and lower FVC/DLCO across SSc, MCTD, IIM, RA and SLE, "especially when Raynaud phenomenon was present". Further research is called for. Review. PMID 39941292; DOI 10.3390/diagnostics15030362 — [Diagnostics 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39941292/)
- SLE: the scleroderma NVC pattern relates to **PAH** risk (see 37794618), not specifically to ILD.

### Inferences
- In RA-ILD, the UIP-predominant, epithelial/IPF-like phenotype makes a vascular-driver mechanism less plausible. Lower density in UIP (25849052) and in IPF (20547086) may reflect age, smoking, hypoxaemia or general fibrotic disease rather than CTD-specific vasculopathy.
- Sensible stratification: (a) SSc, the core population; (b) DM, including anti-MDA5 (microhaemorrhage/loss linked to fibrosis and death); (c) ASS, MCTD and UCTD with Raynaud's as exploratory; (d) RA, SLE and Sjögren's as exploratory or a negative-control-like comparison.

### Gaps
- Anti-MDA5 RP-ILD is a rapidly progressive, possibly vasculopathic and inflammatory process. No retrieved study tests NVC as a predictor of RP-ILD *beyond* ferritin, KL-6 or CT score.
- No retrieved MCTD-ILD study linking NVC to ILD progression.
- No retrieved RA-ILD longitudinal NVC study.

## Q5. Counter-evidence: epithelial-driven paradigm, and dissociation of vasculopathy from fibrosis (lcSSc/ACA vs dcSSc/anti-Scl-70)

### Takeaway
Several strands argue against nailfold damage being a specific upstream driver of lung fibrosis:
- Epithelial injury markers (KL-6) independently predict decline.
- Epithelial αvβ6/TGF-β activation is a mechanistic alternative.
- Human single-cell data centre fibrosis on fibroblasts and macrophages.
- Vascular-dominant phenotypes (ACA+, lcSSc, GAVE, PAH) carry *less* ILD, while dcSSc and anti-Scl-70 carry more ILD and also more severe capillary loss. This makes NVC partly a marker of subset and severity (confounding).
- Some longitudinal studies show no NVC–PFT progression link.
- IPF and IPAH also show nailfold changes, so they are not CTD-specific.

### Cited Findings
- Epithelial paradigm: dysregulated lung epithelium can drive fibrosis by locally activating TGF-β via the integrin αvβ6, which is induced on injured alveolar epithelium. It is proposed as a therapeutic target in SSc-ILD. Review/hypothesis. PMID 22013449; DOI 10.1155/2011/208219 — [Int J Rheumatol 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22013449/)
- KL-6 (type II pneumocyte injury) predicted DLCO decline in two SSc cohorts (n=189, n=118), including on multivariable analysis within milder and more severe strata. PMID 33336433; DOI 10.1111/resp.13988 — [Respirology 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33336433/)
- A review comparing IPF and SSc pathogenesis covers fibroblasts, fibrocytes, myofibroblasts, endothelial, alveolar epithelial and immune cells, with shared and distinct mechanisms. Review. PMID 25726561; DOI 10.1183/09059180.00003214 — [Eur Respir Rev 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25726561/)
- Single-cell: SSc-ILD lungs are dominated by fibrotic (myofibroblast) changes, with vasculopathy mainly in SSc-PAH. PMID 38818750 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38818750/); PMID 31405848 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31405848/)
- Subset confounding: in 50 lcSSc and 55 dcSSc patients, capillary loss was seen in 85.5% of dcSSc vs 48.0% of lcSSc, and lung fibrosis in 72.7% vs 16.0%. Dilated capillaries *without loss* were more common in lcSSc (42% vs 10.9%). Severe capillary loss and ILD therefore co-segregate with the diffuse subset. PMID 16261285; DOI 10.1007/s10067-005-0041-0 — [Clin Rheumatol 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16261285/)
- Vascular-dominant phenotype with less ILD: SSc with GAVE (n=27/269) had more early/active NVC patterns (84.6 vs 62.4%) and more ACA (63 vs 42%), with a trend to *less* ILD (25.9 vs 45.0%, p=0.057) and fewer anti-Scl-70. PMID 42123259; DOI 10.3390/jcm15093526 — [J Clin Med 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42123259/)
- RRI study: higher renal vascular indices with ACA, lcSSc and active/late NVC patterns. ΔRRI predicted PAH but **no other** new complication, ILD included. PMID 32238514 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32238514/)
- Digital ulcers: in the RESCLE registry (n=1,326; 552 with DU), DU history was independently associated with dcSSc and peripheral vascular features but **not with other vascular features such as PH** (abstract truncated beyond this). PMID 27312381; DOI 10.1016/j.semarthrit.2016.04.007 — [Semin Arthritis Rheum 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27312381/)
- No NVC–progression association: 79 SSc, 24 months (see Q3). PMID 34741001 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34741001/)
- Non-specificity: NVC changes occur in IPF (PMID 20547086 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20547086/)), IPAH (PMID 28619664 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28619664/)) and sarcoidosis (tortuosity and crossing capillaries; PMID 36964448; DOI 10.1007/s10067-023-06582-z — [Clin Rheumatol 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36964448/)).
- Overlap syndromes: similar lung involvement despite fewer scleroderma NVC patterns, and no NVC–organ link. PMID 38322100 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38322100/)
- **[ANIMAL]** In the Fra-2 model the upstream driver is Treg deficiency and Th2 skewing, with vascular remodelling and fibrosis as parallel outcomes. PMID 35255201 — [link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35255201/)

### Inferences
- The ACA/lcSSc phenotype (prominent macrovascular/PAH, GAVE, dilated capillaries without loss, little ILD) shows that microvascular abnormality per se is not sufficient for lung fibrosis. Capillary *loss* (density, avascular areas) and the active/late pattern, not giant or dilated capillaries, are the more fibrosis-relevant features. The grant should prespecify density, avascular areas and microhaemorrhages and not rely on "any abnormality".
- Because dcSSc and anti-Scl-70 drive both capillary loss and ILD, any prognostic model must adjust for subset, autoantibody, disease duration and baseline HRCT extent. Otherwise NVC may proxy for these.
- A balanced framing: "systemic microvasculopathy (read by NVC) reflects an active, partly shared pathogenic process (immune-mediated endothelial injury, EndMT, angiogenic imbalance) that runs in parallel with, and may amplify, epithelial and fibroblast-driven fibrosis. Hence NVC *may* carry information on disease activity not captured by a static fibrosis score." This is testable and fits the evidence better than "NVC reflects the upstream driver".

### Gaps
- No retrieved study directly compares NVC-predicted ILD progression in ACA+ vs anti-Scl-70+ strata.
- No retrieved study on anti-RNA polymerase III, NVC and ILD.
- No retrieved study combining NVC and epithelial markers (KL-6, SP-D) in one model to test independence. PMID 40600932 measured both, but its adjustment is unclear from the abstract.
